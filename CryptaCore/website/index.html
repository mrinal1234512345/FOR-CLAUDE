Blockchain-Powered Verification & Cryptographic Provenance

What is CryptaCore?

CryptaCore is a blockchain-based platform that transforms traditional digital records into tamper-evident, verifiable assets with complete ownership history and cryptographic proof.

  • Tamper-Evident Blockchain: Records are immutably stored with cryptographic hashing
  • Smart Contract Automation: Automated verification, approvals, and transfers
  • Digital Ownership: Clear chain of custody and transfer history
  • Wallet Authentication: Secure identity verification via cryptographic wallets
  • IPFS Integration: Decentralized storage for large documents
  • Role-Based Access: Granular permissions for different stakeholders

[ 03 // PROTOCOL LIFECYCLE ]

How CryptaCore Operates

A seamless 4-step cryptographic lifecycle securing records from issuance to verification

01

Register Record

Issuing authority submits record metadata (certificate, license, or digital deed) to the CryptaCore platform.

02

Compute Proof

The system generates a cryptographic SHA-256 digest and anchors the metadata and IPFS CID to the ledger.

03

Anchor to Contract

Smart contracts enforce immutable validation rules, ownership attribution, and multi-signature authorization.

04

Universal Verify

Anyone can authenticate authenticity, validity status, and provenance history in milliseconds via QR or hash.
